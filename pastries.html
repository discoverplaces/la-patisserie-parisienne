Popular French Pastries

Discover some of the most famous and delicious pastries found in Parisian pâtisseries and cafés.

Discover the Beauty of French Pâtisserie

French pastries are famous around the world for their elegance, delicate textures and rich flavours. From buttery croissants to colourful macarons, French pâtisserie combines creativity, tradition and craftsmanship.

Each pastry has its own story, ingredients and preparation method. Some are enjoyed daily in cafés, while others are served as luxury desserts in famous Parisian pastry shops.

Croissant

The croissant is one of the most iconic French pastries. It is known for its flaky, buttery layers and golden colour. A well-made croissant is light, crisp on the outside and soft inside.

In France, croissants are commonly enjoyed for breakfast with coffee, hot chocolate or fresh orange juice.

Discover Croissant

Macaron

Macarons are colourful almond-based pastries filled with buttercream, ganache or jam. They are admired for their smooth shells, soft filling and elegant appearance.

They come in many flavours and colours, making them one of the most attractive pastries displayed in Parisian pâtisseries.

Discover Macaron

Éclair

Éclairs are made using choux pastry and filled with smooth cream before being topped with chocolate, coffee glaze or colourful icing.

Modern pâtisseries in Paris often create éclairs with creative flavours, decorative toppings and artistic designs, turning a classic pastry into a luxury dessert.

Discover Éclair

Paris-Brest

Paris-Brest is a traditional French pastry inspired by the famous Paris-Brest bicycle race. Its round shape represents a bicycle wheel.

It is made from choux pastry and filled with rich praline cream, giving it a nutty, creamy and luxurious flavour.

Discover Paris-Brest
